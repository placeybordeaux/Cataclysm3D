# Bright Nights thermal injuries

Cold exposure tracks frostbite independently for mouth, hands, and feet. BN's Fahrenheit/mph risk zones, clothing warmth, wetness, material wind resistance, covered area, and the existing 34°C cold threshold determine exposure. Low-risk exposure caps at frostnip; severe frostbite thaws through defrosting before clearing. Adjacent radiant heat accelerates recovery. Timers survive character save/load; old saves default to zero timers.

Radiant fire heat can cause blisters. Ambient temperature alone cannot. Material fire resistance, environmental seal, natural armor, and BLISTER_COUNT enchantments (including the activated thermal dissipation CBM) protect covered parts. Injuries use existing data-driven effects for pain, damage, and stat penalties. Minor blister areas map to the existing main-part injury model.

Body temperature and injury refresh run once per game-clock second before effect processing. Sustained heat refreshes blisters without repeated fresh-injury messages, and bulk updates process every damage tick. Environment wind is already zero inside shelter. This preserves the existing six-part body-temperature approximation; individual finger/toe temperatures and BN clothing damage remain outside this port.

Climate control follows BN `Character::temp_corrected_by_climate_control`. It applies to each part's convergent temperature before bonus warmth. Above 37 °C it subtracts the `CLIMATE_CONTROL_COOLING_<PART>` enchantment, below 37 °C it adds `_HEATING_<PART>`, and the result never crosses 37 °C. Being in climate control adds 2.5 °C either way. That covers a worn `CLIMATE_CONTROL` item, `M_SKIN3` asleep on fungus, and an enclosed tile of a vehicle with engine power. Enchantment amounts are legacy units, 500 per °C. A running power armor exoskeleton gives 4 °C on the limbs and 8 °C on the torso and head: BN's value tree counts the plain `CLIMATE_CONTROL` twice there. The cooling suits and RM13 give 2.5 °C. Hands, feet, eyes and mouth have no temperature of their own in this port, so their per-part values have no effect. Details and sources: [power armor, relic effects and climate control](BN_POWER_ARMOR.md#relic-passive-effects-and-climate-control). Tests: `tests/relicEnchant.test.ts`. A running exoskeleton keeps you "Comfortable" at −20 °C and in 30 °C sun, where the switched-off one does not.

Source references: BN character.cpp apply_frostbite/apply_blisters/update_bodytemp, wind_resistance_from_item_list; item.cpp fire_resist/wind_resist. The extracted BN effect and material definitions remain authoritative.

Validation: 75 targeted thermal/body/character/mutation-bionic tests, typecheck, and production build. Regressions cover real winter/fireproof gloves, wet clothing, severe frostbite save/load, warm and radiant recovery, message stability, active/deactivated heatsink, and Character.update(60) versus sixty one-second sustained-heat updates with identical HP, pain, and effect state.
