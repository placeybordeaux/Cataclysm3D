# Bright Nights ally food and rest

Source: read-only Cataclysm-BN `src/npcmove.cpp` address_needs (2209–2280), npc_sleep (1271–1315), rate_food/consume_food (4242–4370); `src/itype.h` get_default_nutr (196–204); `src/npc.cpp` default ally rules (allow_sleep cleared). Existing extracted `TALK_MISC_RULES` supplies the sleep permission UI.

Loaded player allies now consume actual carried food or drink. Extreme hunger/thirst can interrupt a danger response; ordinary eating requires no visible foe. Food ranking follows BN's nutrition conversion, impending spoilage preference, health/taste penalties and waste avoidance. Rotten, parasitic and use-action food/drugs are excluded. Existing inventory consumption owns portion removal, containers, nutrition, thirst and effects; no fabricated food or hunger reset is used. Consuming reserves action time, preventing movement or further consumption until it finishes.

Tired allies rest with permission, when the player is resting, or beyond massive fatigue. They select a better empty, loaded and reachable sleeping spot within six tiles, then use the normal 30-minute lying_down effect and Character sleep/recovery checks. A visible enemy interrupts lying down; actual sleep remains subject to existing waking/attack behavior. Comfort is refreshed during Character updates, including after load. The existing miscellaneous rules responses toggle persisted allow_sleep; old saves default to stay awake.

The old 'schedules' claim describes a broader gap than the BN source supports: this source contains needs decisions and follow/guard roles, not a calendar timetable. The first delivery implements the loaded ally subset. The next source-backed correction adds represented can_eat/will_eat refusals and the explicit BN non-ally supply/fatigue approximation. Instance food poison, offscreen needs, other ally rules, helpers and recruitment remain separate work. Known poisonous flags are conservatively rejected; this does not claim complete poison fidelity.

Validation: typecheck; 66 NPC/Character checks including real extracted menu responses, old/new save compatibility, actual contained water charge conservation, food ingestion/cooldown, no-supply hunger preservation, comfortable-bed movement and danger interruption. Interactive evidence uses actual OS Shift+C, 2, 3 to open the existing ally/miscellaneous menu and select permission. The response becomes Stay awake and npcJSON records true. After OS Backspace exits, the ally naturally moves to a comfortable bench, lies down and sleeps (no direct sleep command). Screenshots were inspected:

OS conversation exit resumed autonomous AI: messages recorded two eats/two drinks, stomach increased by 168 kcal, thirst fell 200→100, no food/water remained carried, and the empty bottle remained owned. No direct consume action or fake event was used. This playtest found a stale rest goal after conversation wake; the corrected rest action rechecks fatigue/permission and conversation wake clears the old goal. Regression verification covers that correction.


Follow-up: `Character::can_eat/will_eat` (`src/consumption.cpp` 651–804) supplies noninteractive refusals. NPC selection now rejects mutation dietary vitamin blacklists, trait food allergies, non-cannibal human flesh, Mycus/proboscis/species restrictions, represented material restrictions, required missing tools, dirty content, nausea, bloating and stomach-inclusive calorie/quench overfill. Unsupported NPC underwater state, instance dirty/poison state and full enchantment-based effective nutrient adjustments remain fidelity limits; autonomous rotten food is already excluded by rate_food as BN does, regardless of those enchantment overrides.

Non-allies now also try their actual carried portions first. When no eligible portion exists, BN consume_food explicitly resets non-allies' stored kcal/thirst, and address_needs resets tired non-allies' fatigue outside danger rather than putting them into ally sleep AI. Those approximations are reproduced as direct source-defined state changes, with no item creation or achievement events. Ally supplies and fatigue never receive these resets. This is not an offscreen calendar routine. Regression checks verify the non-ally/ally distinction, refused portions retained, actual extracted carnivore/wheat/milk/human-flesh restrictions, nausea and stomach overfill. 68 NPC/Character checks and typecheck pass.

Conservation correction: needs inventory traversal prunes every unfinished craft subtree, including a craft inside a normal carried bag. Escrowed foods cannot be consumed and escrowed tools cannot satisfy a comestible's required tool. npcWillEat explicitly refuses isCraft as BN can_eat does. Normal food in ordinary bags and water in bottles remain usable. The focused regression preserves NPC UID, craft object/component identity, complete serialized progress/batch/components, and component counts while consuming separate ordinary contents; a synthetic required-tool food variant isolates that supported BN field without changing extracted content. 96 NPC/Character/crafting checks and typecheck pass.


Rest routing follow-up: better sleeping spots within six tiles are ranked by comfort, then distance. Direct movement stays cheap; blocked direct paths use loaded-only A* with complete-path checks. Searches are capped at 500 nodes each and share 1000 expanded nodes per NPC-system update, including later rest-path refreshes in moveToward. Generic follow/guard/combat path budgets are unchanged. If a rest search cannot produce a complete route, the NPC reconsiders instead of walking a partial stale route. Occupied or newly blocked sleeping tiles cancel the approach. BN's in-place rest fallback remains available.

99 NPC/Character/crafting checks and typecheck verify a detour around a five-tile wall, no generated chunks in isolated NPC simulation, a newly occupied bed cancelling approach, and an initially selected route being closed while the opposite route remains open.
