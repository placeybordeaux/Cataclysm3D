# Bright Nights mutation melee attacks

Read-only source: Cataclysm-BN `src/melee.cpp` mutation_attack_damage (435–446), primary special-attack ordering (1656–1683), perform_special_attacks (3100–3128), hardcoded_mutation_attack (3240–3299), mutation_attacks (3300–3380); `src/creature.cpp` deal_damage_handle_type applies damage_multiplier after absorption. Extracted mutation definitions supply the actual attacks and actor/target messages.

Successful primary player and NPC melee blows now generate mutation attack procs. The declared body part must allow natural attacks through worn coverage; required/blocker mutations select the correct variant. Proc chance uses dexterity plus unarmed skill. Each strike has its own 0.8 hit roll and dodge, independent armor application and source attribution. Specials precede primary damage, stop when the target dies, do not add another swing cost and train unarmed at most once per combo. A primary miss or AoE follow-up does not generate them.

Declared damage preserves negative base contributions before adding strength damage, capped at STR15. The extracted hardcoded beak, tentacle and vine attacks use BN's bounded hit count, working-arm/two-handed/RESTRICT_HANDS restriction, floating-point strength divisions, and post-armor damage multipliers. Unknown hardcoded attacks warn and produce no substitute damage. Player versus monsters, NPC versus monsters, and player/NPC versus characters all consume the same generated packets; NPC kills do not become player kills.

Armor audit: melee results now retain rawDamage for character defenders, including primary crit/skill/technique penetration and multipliers. Monster damage remains the separate post-armor output. Character inventory absorption scales mitigation by damage_multiplier because this port stores already-multiplied amounts. This prevents discarding primary armor metadata or applying monster armor twice. Existing projectile callers do not carry this optional multiplier and are unchanged; their broader fidelity remains separate work.

Validation: 120 mutation/NPC/combat/inventory/mutation-bionic checks, typecheck and production build pass. Coverage includes actual fang variants and mouth coverage, proc distribution, separate dodge, negative antler base damage, STR cap, tentacle/vine armor behavior and restrictions, primary miss/AoE exclusion, primary critical and technique armor metadata, actual player-to-NPC damage, and NPC special-attack kills without player credit.

Interactive verification used a real browser and OS left mouse input. The initial fixture added FANGS and dexterity40 to an unarmed player and supplied a loaded, downed zombie with bash/cut100 and stab15 armor, HP100. No attack helper or fabricated event was invoked. Actual mouse attack reduced target HP100→95; the primary punch dealt no damage. OS Shift+P opened the real log, displaying “You sink your fangs into the zombie for 5 damage” followed by the no-damage primary hit. Anatomy follow-up (2026-10-09). Each strike now rolls its hit and dodge only when it is applied (`MutationStrike.roll()`), right before its body part, block and armor, as in BN perform_special_attacks. A dodge or block spent on one strike is gone for the next, and unarmed training happens on the first strike that lands. Bodies already use BN's full human anatomy: 12 parts (`src/game/bodyparts.ts`), with HP on the six main parts like BN. Strikes on people pick from all 12 by BN hit weights, including the eyes. So the earlier "six-part" note was not a gap.

Venom (BN melee.cpp 1685–1697): with POISONOUS (checked first) or POISONOUS2, cut/stab damage dealt by the last mutation strike that landed, or by an unarmed primary blow, adds `poison` / `badpoison` for 6 turns. Only the survivor gets the message ("You poison …!" / "You inject your venom into …!"), and it shows even when the target is immune. Monsters take poison only if they are WARM and made of flesh/iflesh (monster::is_immune_effect). On monsters, badpoison gives −10 speed, −2 dodge and −2 melee accuracy, and HURT rolls one_in(60) per turn for 1–2 damage. Plain poison only hurts, one_in(450) for 1. People get the ordinary effect from data. Real-input check: `tools/realinput.ts --only venom` (bite on a zombie logs the venom line, and the zombie is 10 speed slower).

Limits: the badpoison melee bash/cut penalty on monsters is not modelled. No additional special-attack animation is made. Ranged bionic guns are a separate package: see [BN_BIONIC_GUNS.md](BN_BIONIC_GUNS.md).

Passive martial-arts armor also subtracts before BN's final damage multiplier (`character.cpp` passive_absorb_hit, 9392). The follow-up scales that subtraction for this port's already-folded damage units. A guard3 plus natural armor2 against raw bash10 multiplied by2 deals10, preserving both distinct absorption steps exactly once; ordinary multiplier-free monster packets are unchanged.
